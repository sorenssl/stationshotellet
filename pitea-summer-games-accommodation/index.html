Twice every summer Piteå fills up with visitors. A whole apartment with its own kitchen is then often both cheaper and more comfortable than several hotel rooms — especially for a group.

Piteå Summer Games

Piteå Summer Games is one of Sweden's largest youth football tournaments, with hundreds of teams from around the world. It is played the weekend after Midsummer on pitches in and around Piteå — and thanks to the midnight sun, matches run late into the evening. Exact dates and schedules are at piteasummergames.se.

Piteå Dansar och Ler (PDOL)

PDOL is Piteå's big town festival at the end of July, with concerts and a funfair in the centre — and free entry. Programme and dates are at pdol.se.

Why a whole apartment for the events?

  • Sleeps 5 in two bedrooms — one price for the whole apartment, not per person.
  • Your own kitchen — breakfast and dinner at home instead of eating out every meal.
  • Washing machine — kits clean for the next match.
  • Free private parking — no hunting for a space in town during festival week.
  • Self check-in with a key box — arrive when you arrive.
