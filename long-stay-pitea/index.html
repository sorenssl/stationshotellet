Prices are for the whole apartment, up to 5 people — not per person. A crew of five sharing for a month pays 4,760 kr per person per month. Linens, towels, Wi-Fi and parking are included. The price you see is the price you pay.

Why it works for a work assignment

  • Two bedrooms, sleeps 5 — colleagues can share without sharing a room with strangers.
  • Fully equipped kitchen — cook your own meals instead of eating out every night. ICA and Coop in Öjebyn are within walking distance.
  • Washing machine — clean work clothes without a laundromat.
  • Free private parking, close to the E4.
  • Self check-in with a key box — arrive when your shift ends; the code is emailed before arrival.
  • In-room safe and a separate shower room.

Location — close to work in Piteå

  • Central Piteå: about 5 minutes by car.
  • Piteå hospital: about 5 km.
  • Markbygden wind farm area: west of Piteå, a little over 30 km as the crow flies to the centre of the area.
  • Luleå Airport: about 50 km, 40–45 minutes via the E4.

Cancellation

Weekly stays: free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Monthly stays: one month's deposit, fully refunded if you cancel 30 days or more before arrival. Full details in our booking terms.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can stay?

Up to 5 people, in two bedrooms.

Is cleaning included on a monthly stay?

Yes, weekly cleaning is included in the monthly rate.

Can I check in late at night?

Yes. Self check-in with a key box from 15:00, so you can arrive whenever suits you. Check-out by 11:00.

Is there parking?

Yes, private parking is included at no extra cost.
